# Following up

- Wait at least two working days before the first follow-up
- Keep it to two or three lines: remind what I asked, why it matters, and by when
- Never sound annoyed or guilt-trip ("just bumping this", "as per my last email")
- Make it easy to answer: a yes/no question or two options
- After a second follow-up with no answer, suggest a call or another person to ask
